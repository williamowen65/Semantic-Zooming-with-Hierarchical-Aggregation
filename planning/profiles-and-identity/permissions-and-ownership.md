# Profiles and Identity — Permissions and Ownership

## Purpose

Define authority over profiles and the relationship between identity and content ownership/stewardship.

## Questions to Resolve

- Who can edit profile metadata?
- Who can add/remove root Nodes from a profile?
- Does creating a Node imply ownership, authorship, stewardship, or only provenance?
- Can ownership/stewardship be transferred or shared?
- Is pseudonymous or anonymous participation supported?
